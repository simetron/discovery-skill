## 2. Interview guide

```
Participant (and why they match the segment):
Date / interviewers (who asks, who takes notes):

Goal: (one of the top-3 unknowns)

Opening (2 min): thank them, explain you are learning about [topic], not selling.

Story prompt: "Tell me about the last time you [did X]."
Follow-ups:
- What happened right before / after?
- What did you do? What did you use?
- What else did you try?
- How long did it take? What did it cost?

Closing: "Who else should I talk to?" "Is there anything I should have asked?"

Reminders: no pitching, no hypotheticals, stay on one specific event.
```
