# Further reading

Books that go deeper on the practices in this manual:

- **Continuous Discovery Habits**, Teresa Torres. The weekly habit, opportunity solution trees, story-based interviewing and assumption testing in depth.
- **The Mom Test**, Rob Fitzpatrick. A short guide to getting honest answers from customers.
- **Just Enough Research**, Erika Hall. Right-sized research methods, bias and statistical confidence.
- **Inspired**, Marty Cagan. How strong product teams operate and the main discovery techniques they use.
- **Testing Business Ideas**, David J. Bland and Alexander Osterwalder. A large catalog of experiments for testing assumptions.
